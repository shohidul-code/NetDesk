# Opening a complaint

1. Open the app and go to My complaints.
2. Choose a category: no internet, slow speed, billing, or other.
3. Describe the problem: when it started, which lights are on, and what you already tried.
4. Submit. You will see the complaint with the status "open".

# What happens next

- open: the complaint is waiting for a technician.
- in progress: a technician is assigned and working on it.
- resolved: the technician fixed it. Tell the office if the problem comes back.
- closed: the complaint is finished.

Most no-internet complaints are handled within 24 hours. During an area outage, check the notice in the app first; you do not need to open a separate complaint.
