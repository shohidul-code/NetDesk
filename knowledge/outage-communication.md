# When to post a notice

If three or more customers in one area report no internet within one hour, treat it as an area outage.

# Steps

1. Post an area notice in the admin panel within 15 minutes, in Bangla and English, with a realistic end time.
2. Call the field lead and send a technician to the distribution point.
3. Update the notice when the cause is known and again when service returns.

# What not to promise

Do not promise compensation or an exact repair time unless the owner has approved it.
