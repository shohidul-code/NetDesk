# When to restart

If the internet stops working and the app shows no outage notice for your area, restart the router first. It fixes most short drops.

# Steps

1. Unplug the router's power cable. Leave the ONU (the small fibre box) on.
2. Wait 30 seconds.
3. Plug the router back in.
4. Wait 2 minutes for the lights to settle, then try a website.

# If it still does not work

Check the lights on the ONU. A red LOS light means a fibre fault: do not pull the cable yourself, open a complaint in the app instead. Otherwise open a complaint with the category "no internet".
