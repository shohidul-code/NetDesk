# Before you move

Tell the office at least 3 days before you move. Give your customer code and the new address.

# Rules

- Shifting is possible only inside our service areas: Mirpur 10, Uttara Sector 7 and Mohammadpur.
- The shifting fee is ৳500, paid with the next bill.
- All earlier bills must be paid first.
- A technician moves the ONU and router and lays new cable if needed. Up to 30 metres of cable is free.

# Outside our areas

If the new address is outside our areas, the connection is closed after the last bill is paid. Return the ONU to the office.
