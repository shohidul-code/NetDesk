# Check before you complain

1. Run a speed test on a device connected with a LAN cable. Wi-Fi speed is always lower than the package speed.
2. Count the devices using the connection. Video streaming and downloads on several phones share one package.
3. Sit closer to the router. Walls and floors weaken Wi-Fi a lot.
4. Restart the router.
5. Check the app for an outage or maintenance notice in your area.

# Evening slowdown

Between 8 pm and 11 pm many customers are online. A small drop is normal. If the speed falls below half of your package speed on a LAN cable, open a complaint with the category "slow speed".

# What to write in the complaint

Write the speed test result, the time, and whether you used a cable or Wi-Fi. This helps the technician find the cause faster.
