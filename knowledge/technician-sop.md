# Before the visit

1. Open the complaint queue and set the complaint to in progress.
2. Call the customer to confirm someone is at home.
3. Check the area notices; do not visit for a known area outage.

# At the site

1. Check the ONU PON and LOS lights and measure optical power. Below -27 dBm means a fibre fault.
2. Check the patch cord, the LAN cable and the router WAN settings.
3. Replace a faulty router only with a stock router and record its serial number.

# After the visit

Write what you found and did in the complaint, then set it to resolved. Never share one customer's details with another.
