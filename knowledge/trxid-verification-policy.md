# What a TrxID is

Every bKash or Nagad payment gets a transaction ID (TrxID), for example 9KD3XH21PQ. It is the proof of your payment.

# How we verify

1. You send the TrxID to the office with your customer code.
2. Staff check the TrxID and the amount in the bKash or Nagad merchant account.
3. When it matches, the bill is marked paid. This usually takes one working day.

# Rules

- One TrxID can pay only one bill. A TrxID that was already used is rejected.
- If you paid two months at once, tell the office; staff will split it.
- Cash payments get a printed receipt instead of a TrxID.
- Keep the TrxID SMS until the bill shows as paid.
